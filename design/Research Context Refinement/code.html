Lexical Entrainment

The cognitive process by which conversational partners unconsciously begin to align their vocabularies and syntactic structures. In this stream, noted specifically around technical jargon.

Semantic Prosody

The nuance or underlying 'flavor' (often positive or negative) that a seemingly neutral word acquires through frequent co-occurrence with specific collocates in the source dialect.
